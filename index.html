About this data. Figures are Total-area percentages from NFHS-5 (2019–2021). High Blood Sugar and Elevated Blood Pressure cover age 15+; Overweight/Obesity covers age 15–49. Higher reported levels do not mean a state is "unhealthier" — they may reflect differences in healthcare access, testing rates, or urbanization.
